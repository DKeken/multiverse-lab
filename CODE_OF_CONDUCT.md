# Code of conduct

Be precise, respectful, and evidence-first. Critique claims and methods, not people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, credential sharing, unsafe experimentation, and repeated promotion of unsupported extraordinary claims are not accepted.

Scientific disagreement is expected. State assumptions, sources, uncertainty, and counterexamples. Negative results and corrections are valued.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through GitHub. Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and research integrity.
