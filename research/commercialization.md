# Commercialization before speculative breakthrough

## Primary wedge: CAS-CI for computational science

A command/CI check that accepts restricted hypothesis equations and returns:

- strict schema, unit, domain, bounds, and cost-gate failures;
- deterministic normalized artifact and content hash;
- local SymPy derivation report;
- independent official Wolfram agreement/disagreement with exact provenance;
- immutable audit event suitable for paper supplements and regulated model review.

Current evidence: compiler, verifier, archive, scheduler, and official Wolfram identity gate run locally. Current non-capabilities: arbitrary mathematics, proof of physical reality, publication-grade cosmology inference, portal feasibility, hosted multi-user service, and customer integrations.

### Why this wedge

It reuses shipped code and needs no new scientific result, GPU, large dataset, database, or custom UI. Existing alternatives cover adjacent pieces—unit packages, CAS notebooks, workflow engines, and data provenance—but the product hypothesis is that teams will pay for a narrow fail-closed cross-CAS review trail in pull requests. Willingness to pay is **unknown** until interviews or an LOI.

### 30-day validation, requiring approval before external contact

1. Prepare one local CLI demo against three public scientific repositories with intentionally seeded unit/domain/assumption errors.
2. Ask 15 authorized computational-science or model-risk users for a 20-minute workflow interview.
3. Offer a two-week design-partner trial; ask for an LOI or paid pilot, not compliments.
4. Pass: at least 3 teams provide real equations and at least 1 signs LOI/paid pilot at declared price.
5. Kill/pivot: fewer than 3 real workflow submissions or zero LOI after 15 qualified interviews.

No outreach occurs without human approval.

## Fallback: reproducible anomaly-search audit trail

Package frozen masks, simulations, trials ledgers, and holdout rules for astronomy groups. It has stronger domain differentiation but slower sales, larger data, publication liability, and field-specific integration. Do not market automated discovery.

## Moat hypothesis

Not model prompts. Defensible asset is a growing, permissioned corpus of real failure signatures, normalized scientific IR, CAS disagreements, unit/domain counterexamples, and reproducible gate manifests. This remains a hypothesis until customers contribute data under explicit rights.

## Boundaries

- No claim that CAS verification proves a physical statement.
- No automated `empirically_supported` promotion.
- No medical, safety-critical, weapons, export-controlled, or regulatory certification claim.
- No customer equation/data retention without contract and access controls.
- No multiverse or portal branding in product claims; research narrative is separate.
