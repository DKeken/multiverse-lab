# Governance

Multiverse Lab uses maintainer review with evidence-gated scientific authority.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose code, data manifests, hypotheses, reproductions, and falsifiers.
- **Reviewers** assess code/security or scientific evidence within declared expertise.
- **Maintainers** merge changes, manage releases, freeze holdouts, and resolve contract conflicts.

Initial maintainer: [DKeken](https://github.com/DKeken).

## Decisions

Routine changes use pull-request consensus. Changes to status semantics, evidence thresholds, licenses, security boundaries, holdout access, or empirical claims require maintainer approval and a decision-log entry.

No model, CAS, simulation, contributor count, or vote can promote a claim to `empirically_supported`. Promotion requires the observation contract, global calibration, independent holdout, provenance, and human review.

## Conflicts of interest

Authors disclose financial, institutional, dataset-access, and analysis-design conflicts relevant to a claim. A maintainer with a material conflict should request independent review.

## Releases

Releases freeze code, environment lock, dataset registry, hashes, seeds, thresholds, evidence ledger, and known limitations. Retractions and corrected results remain visible; history is not rewritten.

## Community changes

After three active independent contributors, governance may add reviewers by documented maintainer decision. After two maintainers exist, changes to governance require two-maintainer approval.
